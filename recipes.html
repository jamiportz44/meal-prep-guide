Easy meal prep ideas

These are flexible starter ideas rather than strict recipes. Adjust portions and ingredients to fit your household and preferences.

🥣

Overnight oats

Combine oats with milk or a suitable alternative in a covered container. Refrigerate overnight and add fruit or toppings before eating.

Prep tip: prepare individual portions for a grab-and-go breakfast.

🍚

Build-your-own grain bowl

Start with a cooked grain, add vegetables and a protein, then finish with a dressing, salsa, or sauce.

Prep tip: store wet toppings separately to help maintain texture.

🥦

Sheet-pan vegetables

Cut vegetables into similar-sized pieces, toss with a little oil and seasoning, and roast until cooked to your liking.

Prep tip: choose vegetables with similar cooking times for easier preparation.

🥪

Lunchbox snack plate

Pair crackers or pita with cheese or hummus, fruit, and cut vegetables for a mix-and-match lunch.

Prep tip: keep refrigerated items chilled until it is time to eat.

🌮

Flexible taco filling

Season beans, lentils, tofu, or cooked meat and serve with tortillas, greens, salsa, and other favorite toppings.

Prep tip: prepare filling ahead and assemble tacos when serving.

🍝

Simple pasta bowls

Combine pasta with tomato sauce, vegetables, and a protein of choice for an adaptable lunch or dinner.

Prep tip: portion sauce and pasta separately if you prefer a different texture.

Keep it personal: Choose meals you enjoy and ingredients you can realistically use. The best plan is one you will want to follow.
