Start with the basics

Meal prep basics for beginners

A repeatable routine can make meal planning less stressful. You do not need special equipment or a full day of cooking to get started.

A 45-minute planning routine

  1. Check what you have. Look through the fridge, freezer, and pantry before making a list.
  2. Choose a few meals. Pick two or three options that share ingredients.
  3. Write a focused list. Group items by produce, protein, grains, and pantry staples.
  4. Prep the building blocks. Wash produce, chop vegetables, cook a grain, or make a sauce.
  5. Pack and label. Use clean containers and mark them with the preparation date.

Keep food safety in the plan

Chill promptly

Refrigerate perishable food promptly rather than leaving it at room temperature for extended periods.

Use trusted guidance

Storage times depend on the food. Check current guidance from the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service instead of guessing.

USDA food safety resources ↗

Reheat safely

Use a food thermometer and follow official recommendations for reheating leftovers. Avoid relying on smell or appearance alone.

Make it manageable: If a full meal-prep session feels like too much, start with one task—such as preparing breakfast for two days or chopping vegetables for dinner.
