Plan ahead, stay flexible

A simple weekly meal plan

This sample plan uses a short list of ingredients across several meals. Swap ingredients to suit your preferences, budget, and what you already have at home.

How to use it: Pick the meals you actually want to eat, check your pantry first, then shop for only what is missing.
Lunch

Monday & Tuesday: Grain bowls

Cooked rice or quinoa, roasted vegetables, chickpeas or chicken, and a simple dressing.

Prep task: cook the grain and roast vegetables.

Lunch

Wednesday & Thursday: Wraps

Whole-wheat wraps with leftover protein, greens, chopped vegetables, and hummus or dressing.

Prep task: wash and chop vegetables; assemble close to eating time.

Dinner

Monday: Sheet-pan dinner

Roast a protein of choice with potatoes and seasonal vegetables on a sheet pan.

Prep task: chop vegetables and season ingredients.

Dinner

Tuesday: Quick bean tacos

Beans, tortillas, cabbage or lettuce, salsa, and any leftover roasted vegetables.

Prep task: keep toppings in separate containers.

Dinner

Wednesday: Pasta night

Pasta with tomato sauce, spinach, and beans or another protein of choice.

Prep task: portion ingredients; cook pasta when ready to eat.

Flexible

Thursday & Friday: Leftover remix

Combine remaining grains, vegetables, and protein into a bowl, wrap, soup, or fried-rice-style dish.

Prep task: check stored food and use it safely.

Build a short shopping list

  • Grains: rice or quinoa, pasta, tortillas or wraps
  • Produce: potatoes, leafy greens, seasonal vegetables, cabbage, and any preferred extras
  • Protein: beans or chickpeas, plus chicken, tofu, eggs, or another preferred option
  • Flavor: tomato sauce, salsa, hummus, dressing, herbs, and seasonings
